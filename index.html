02 — What we do

Five things, done properly.

01

TV commercials & brand films

Concept to delivered master. We assemble and run the full unit — director, DOP, 1st/2nd AD, gaffer, art department, stylists, casting — and hand back a finished film.

02

Commercial & product photography

Studio product work with food and prop styling, shot and retouched in-house. Reflection control, surface and colour work on difficult materials — glass, plastic, liquid.

03

Digital unit & social content

The second camera on set that pays for itself — BTS, vertical cutdowns, stills and social-first edits captured alongside the main shoot, not bolted on afterwards.

04

Event & conference coverage

Multi-camera coverage of launches, summits and activations, cut fast enough to post while the event is still running. Airshows, tech summits, brand activations.

05

Post-production

Offline and online edit, VFX and motion, grading, retouching, sound design, mix and voice-over — coordinated by us so you deal with one contact, not seven vendors.
