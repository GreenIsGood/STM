Mono, uppercase, wide-tracked section label — the connective tissue of every STM section.

```jsx
<Eyebrow>01 — Selected work</Eyebrow>
<Eyebrow arabic>01 — أعمال مختارة</Eyebrow>
```

Always numbered when it heads a major section (`01 —`, `02 —`). Never bold, never accent-coloured. Arabic never uppercases or tracks out — pass `arabic`.
